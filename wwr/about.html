History

Lieutenant John C. Frémont and inventor Horace H. Day built one of the earliest rubber-based inflatable rafts to survey the Rocky Mountains and the Great Plains via the Platte River. John Wesley Powell and scientific teams led pioneering wooden-boat expeditions through the Green River and Colorado River canyons.
